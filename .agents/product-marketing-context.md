# Product Marketing Context (persistent)

Read this file before writing or editing any customer-facing copy,
microcopy, marketing surface, email template, quiz question, or FAQ
entry. Mirror of `docs/AUDIENCE_AND_POSITIONING.md` kept next to the
agent workspace so it is always available to code tasks.

## Audience (one sentence)

Business owners age 50+ and beginner entrepreneurs who want to use
AI to build websites and simple apps for their business — or to sell
— without becoming a developer.

## Two use cases (never branch the product)

1. Build something for my business.
2. Build websites or apps I can sell.

## Rules for every copy task

- Speak like a patient teacher, not a tech marketer.
- No unexplained jargon (`no-code`, `wireframe`, `user flow`,
  `feature boundary`, `MVP`, `prompt`, `deploy`, `iteration`).
- Explain by outcome, not by feature.
- No profit, income, savings, or client-count guarantee.
- No fake scarcity for a self-paced challenge.
- Only the confirmed AI For Business positioning may render as
  authority proof. All other scale/numeric claims stay in
  `docs/CLAIMS_AND_PROOF.md` until confirmed.
- Single dominant CTA per page: **Start My Free 5-Day Challenge**.
- Essential text ≥14px; body ≥17px where practical.

## Approved plain-English swaps

| Jargon | Plain English |
| --- | --- |
| no-code | You build by describing what you want in everyday English. You won't write code. |
| wireframe | simple sketch of your pages or screens |
| user flow | what someone clicks first, next, and last |
| feature boundary / MVP | the small first version worth building |
| prompt | the instructions you give the AI |
| deploy | put it online / share the link |
| iteration | what to improve next |
